Privacy Policy for Mektra: Ascend
Effective date: September 13, 2026
Mektra: Ascend is published by Mektra (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the mobile game Mektra: Ascend (the “Game”).
Information stored by the Game
The Game does not require an account, registration, sign-in, or submission of your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, or camera data.
Game progress and preferences—such as high scores, unlocked content, selected themes, audio settings, and other gameplay settings—are stored locally on your device using the device’s application storage. We do not receive this locally stored information.
Advertising, payments, and analytics
The Game does not contain third-party advertising and does not use in-game advertising profiles.
The Game does not include an in-game account system or an in-game purchase system. If you purchase the Game through Google Play, Google processes the transaction under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
The Game does not include a developer-operated analytics account or a custom analytics service. The Unity game engine and the Android/Google Play platform may process limited technical or diagnostic information as part of operating, securing, and distributing the Game. Those services process information under their own privacy policies and settings.
How we use information
We use information available to us only to operate, maintain, support, and improve the Game, respond to support requests, comply with legal obligations, and protect the Game and its users.
Sharing
We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers. Service providers such as Google Play or Unity may process technical information when their platform or engine services are used, subject to their own policies.
Children
The Game is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.
Data security and retention
Information stored only on your device is protected by the device and operating system. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain records. We do not maintain a separate player account database for the Game.
Your choices and rights
You can remove locally stored Game data by using your device’s app-storage controls or uninstalling the Game. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights. To ask a privacy question or make a request, contact us through the Mektra GitHub repository:
https://github.com/elishagithub/MektraRunRepo
Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
Contact
For privacy questions about Mektra: Ascend, contact Mektra through the repository above.
